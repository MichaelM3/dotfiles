# Learning Record Format

Learning records live in `learning-records/` and use sequential numbering:
`0001-slug.md`, `0002-slug.md`.

```markdown
# <Short Title>

One to three sentences: what was learned or established, and why it matters for
future sessions.
```

Optional sections:

- `Status: active | superseded by LR-NNNN`
- `## Evidence`
- `## Implications`

Write a record for demonstrated understanding, disclosed prior knowledge,
corrected misconceptions, or mission shifts. Do not record mere coverage.
